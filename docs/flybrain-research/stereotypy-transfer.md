# Research notes: stereotypy and cross-animal transfer (F9, F1)
Written 2026-09-24. Read-only literature pass. Every source below was fetched unless it is marked UNVERIFIED.

## Sources
1. Schlegel et al. 2024, Nature 634:139, doi 10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5 (PMC11446831). "56%; 2,920 out of 5,235" hemibrain types found unambiguously in FlyWire, and "1,651 (32%)" were not reidentified. About 5% of types were split and 7% were merged. Edges with more than 10 synapses reproduce more than 90% of the time. About 16% of edges are above that threshold, and they hold about 79% of synapses. Edge weight correlation is 0.97 within a brain and 0.8 across brains. Cell counts per type differ by 0.3±1.8 within a brain and 0.8±10 across brains. The paper defines a cell type as a group more similar to cells in another brain than to other cells in the same brain. Links: CONFIRMS F1, METHOD F9, EXTENDS F8.
2. Dorkenwald et al. 2024, Nature, doi 10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y. In FlyWire, 93.7% of presynapses and only 44.7% of postsynapses are attached to proofread segments. Links: METHOD F9 (input-degree features are biased). The number comes from a search snippet; the full paper was not fetched.
3. Berg et al. 2025 (male CNS), bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.10.09.680999 and PMC12636603; later published in Cell. "97.5% of neurons across the CNS have a cell type match" to FAFB, hemibrain or MANC. Of 7,319 cross-matched central-brain types, 114 are dimorphic, 262 are male-specific and 69 are female-specific. These make up 4.8% of male neurons and 2.4% of female neurons. Matching used "spatial transforms + NBLAST and connectivity co-clustering". About 4% of FlyWire neurons were revised. Links: CONFIRMS F9.
4. Bates et al. 2025 (BANC), bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.07.31.667571; later published in Nature. "26% of BANC neurons excluding the optic lobes" are unmatched to other datasets. Normalized synapse counts show "strong concordance" with other adult connectomes. Links: EXTENDS F9 (the match set covers only about 74% of neurons).
5. Stürner et al. 2025, Nature 643:158, doi 10.1038/s41586-025-08925-z (PMC12222017; bioRxiv 2024.06.04.596633). Across FAFB, FANC and MANC, 92–99% of DNs and ANs are matched. For DNa02, the FANC/MANC slope is 0.42 on raw counts and 0.69 on normalized counts. "The connection strengths across the two sides of the animal are comparable to the differences seen across the two datasets." About 1–4% of neurons are sex-specific and 2–3% are dimorphic. Links: CONFIRMS F9, METHOD (normalize weights).
6. Matsliah et al. 2026, bioRxiv (PMC13307954). ACDC graph alignment of the male and female VNC. Where connectivity matching and NBLAST disagreed, reviewers judged the connectivity match better in 62% of cases and NBLAST better in 7%. Connectivity matching was as good or better in 88% of cases. The study reports 205 male-specific, 190 female-specific and 144 dimorphic IN types. Links: METHOD F9, CONFIRMS F7.
7. Matsliah et al. 2024, Nature, doi 10.1038/s41586-024-07981-1 (PMC11446827). "A cell type is defined as a set of cells with similar feature vectors based on connectivity." "Membership can be accurately predicted by a logical conjunction of on average five synaptic partner types." Links: CONFIRMS F8 (optic-lobe types are circular), which explains the high ol score in F1.
8. Nern et al. 2025, Nature, doi 10.1038/s41586-025-08746-0 (bioRxiv 2024.04.16.589741). Male optic lobe with 727 types. The claim that more than 98% of types (99.8% of cells) match FlyWire comes from a search snippet only and is UNVERIFIED. Links: EXTENDS F9.
9. Schwartzman et al. 2025, NTAC, bioRxiv 2025.06.11.659184 and Nat Commun (PMC12259112). Seeded mode reaches more than 95% on the optic lobe with 2% of labels and more than 90% on the central brain. Unseeded mode reaches about 70% on the optic lobe and 52% on the whole brain. An NBLAST kNN gets less than 50%. No cross-dataset test was run. Links: CONFIRMS F1, METHOD F9, with a circularity caveat (F8).
10. Eckstein et al. 2024, Cell (PMC11106717). Separate networks were trained per dataset. Synapse-level accuracy is 87% on FAFB and 78% on hemibrain. Neuron-level accuracy is 94% and 91%. Splits were by neuron, with extra splits by neuropil and hemilineage. Monoamine predictions are the least consistent across datasets. Links: CONTEXT F3 (NT labels are predictions).
11. Scheffer 2025, bioRxiv 2025.10.16.682869. FIB-SEM datasets show "more than 40%" more synapses than serial-section TEM. Links: METHOD F9 (normalize features).
12. Winding et al. 2023, Science, doi 10.1126/science.add9330 (bioRxiv 2022.11.28.516756). Cell types come from spectral embedding plus hierarchical clustering of connectivity. 93% of neurons have a contralateral homolog. Edges with at least 5 synapses are more than 80% symmetric, and edges with at least 10 synapses are more than 95% symmetric. Links: CONFIRMS F8 (circularity), CONFIRMS F9 (left/right as a transfer test).
13. Pedigo et al. 2023, eLife 12:e83739. Larval left density is about 0.93 of the right (p<1e-23). A density-adjusted test finds differences only in KC→CN and KC→MBON. Symmetry is restored when Kenyon cells are removed (p≈0.60). Links: METHOD F9 (density shift).
14. Pedigo et al. 2023, Network Neuroscience (bisected graph matching). Pairing accuracy in the larva improves by about 22%. Absolute numbers were not captured. Links: METHOD.
15. Truman et al. 2023, eLife 12:e80594. There are "no larval MBIN-MBON connections being maintained through metamorphosis." Links: CONTRADICTS the idea of larva-to-adult transfer.
16. Witvliet et al. 2021, Nature (bioRxiv 2020.04.30.066209). In C. elegans, about 43% of connections vary between animals. Stable connections average 6.6 synapses and variable ones 1.4. Links: CONTEXT (weak edges are noise).

## Synthesis
- Plausible ceilings. Within one sex, adult brain to brain, about 2/3 of hemibrain types are reproducible (Schlegel). At the super_class and cell_class level, matching is 92–99% (Stürner, Berg).
- A cross-animal classifier should do about as well as the within-animal left/right split for coarse classes. Stürner reports that left/right variance is about the same as cross-dataset variance. Fine types are capped by the 2–5% sex-specific neurons and by labels that do not reproduce.
- Expect a drop at the fine-type level. Guess: a 10–30 point fall from within-dataset accuracy, driven by synapse detection differences (about 40% FIB vs TEM) and FlyWire's low postsynapse attachment. Normalize to fractions and rank features.
- Keep the ground truth for transfer to the reviewed-match subset. That subset excludes the roughly 26% of BANC neurons that are unmatched, so report coverage alongside accuracy.
- Controls to add: a left→right hemisphere transfer within one dataset, as an upper bound on cross-animal transfer; a hemibrain→FlyWire test on the 56% of types that match unambiguously; and dimorphic types reported separately.
- Larva to adult: neither the wiring nor the connection logic survives metamorphosis, so skip this transfer.
- Circularity: optic-lobe and larval types are defined by connectivity, so high F1 scores on them are partly guaranteed. Cross-animal transfer to independently reviewed matches is a less circular test.
